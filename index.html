About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in astrophysics at the Kavli Institute for Astronomy and Astrophysics, Peking University, advised by Prof. Linhua Jiang.

My research focuses on high-redshift galaxies, the Epoch of Reionization, and active galactic nuclei. I use deep imaging and spectroscopy—especially JWST/NIRCam wide-field slitless spectroscopy—to build statistical views of galaxy growth across cosmic time. I am currently visiting the University of Manchester, hosted by Prof. Christopher J. Conselice.

02

Research

From individual emission lines to the evolving galaxy population.

A

Star formation across cosmic time

Measuring hydrogen recombination-line luminosity functions, star formation rate functions, and the star-forming main sequence from the nearby Universe to the Epoch of Reionization.

  • Hα, Hβ, Paschen, and Brackett emission
  • Completeness and Eddington-bias modeling
  • Maximum-likelihood and Bayesian inference

B

Galaxies in the reionization era

Investigating which galaxies supplied the ionizing photons that reionized the Universe, with emphasis on ionizing efficiency, escape fraction, morphology, and environment.

  • Ionizing photon production efficiency
  • Lyα emission and escape
  • Galaxy structure and environment

C

Early black-hole growth

Exploring the nature of active galactic nuclei in the early Universe, including little red dots and the transition toward luminous quasars.

  • AGN spectral energy distributions
  • Broad emission-line diagnostics
  • Galaxy–black-hole coevolution

03

Current work

JWST · MAGNIF

Faint Hα emitters behind gravitational-lensing clusters

Constraining the faint end of the Hα luminosity function and the cosmic star formation rate density at z ≈ 4.5 and 6.3.

JWST · COSMOS-3D

A panoramic emission-line census

Building a catalog of more than 12,000 emission-line galaxies over 0.33 deg², spanning redshifts from the nearby Universe to z ≈ 9.

JWST · COSMOS + GOODS

The galaxies that drove reionization

Connecting star formation, ionizing efficiency, Lyα escape, morphology, and environment across multiple deep fields.
