01

About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in astrophysics at the Kavli Institute for Astronomy and Astrophysics, Peking University, advised by Prof. Linhua Jiang.

My research focuses on high-redshift galaxy formation and evolution, cosmic star-formation history, the Epoch of Reionization, and early black-hole growth. I use deep imaging and spectroscopy—especially JWST/NIRCam wide-field slitless spectroscopy—to build statistical views of galaxy growth across cosmic time. I am currently a visiting Ph.D. student at the Jodrell Bank Centre for Astrophysics, University of Manchester, hosted by Prof. Christopher J. Conselice.

02

Research Interests

From individual emission lines to the evolving galaxy population.

A

Star formation across cosmic time

Measuring hydrogen recombination-line luminosity functions, star formation rate functions, and the star-forming main sequence from the nearby Universe to the Epoch of Reionization.

  • Hα, Hβ, Paschen, and Brackett emission
  • Completeness and Eddington-bias modeling
  • Maximum-likelihood and Bayesian inference

B

Galaxies in the reionization era

Investigating which galaxies supplied the ionizing photons that reionized the Universe, with emphasis on ionizing efficiency, escape fraction, morphology, and environment.

  • Ionizing photon production efficiency
  • Lyα emission and escape
  • Galaxy structure and environment

C

Early black-hole growth

Exploring active galactic nuclei in the early Universe, including little red dots and their transition toward luminous quasars.

  • AGN spectral energy distributions
  • Broad emission-line diagnostics
  • Galaxy–black-hole coevolution
